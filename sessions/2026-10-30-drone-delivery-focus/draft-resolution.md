# Draft resolution

**Recommendation: Option A, run as a managed service. GO WITH CONDITIONS.**

Sell medical delivery (lab samples, pharmacy, hospital-to-hospital runs) to health systems as a done-for-you outcome, priced per run. Subcontract every flight to a certified Part 135 operator that is already approved for the corridor. Buy no aircraft. The software the founder builds is the company's own dispatch, chain-of-custody and billing tool, not the product. Drop the retail lean (B), and drop software sold to operators (D).

## Conditions

1. **Contacts check, by 2026-11-29 (day 30).** The founder names 10 contacts in healthcare who pay to move a specific item today (Graham). If the contacts are mainly in retail or food, stop and re-decide before spending on A.
2. **Operator subcontract, by 2026-12-29 (day 60).** A named Part 135 operator, already approved for a corridor near a target health system, signs a written subcontract at $25 or less per run, with insurance naming the startup (Hormozi on price, Buffett on insurance, Elon on the approved corridor).
3. **Paid pilots, by 2027-01-28 (day 90).** Two health systems sign paid pilots at $50 or more per run, with a monthly minimum of $2,000 (Hormozi on price, Bezos on count, Buffett on minimum). The first should replace an existing ground courier budget, not open a new line item (Hormozi).
4. **Margin.** The subcontract cost stays at or under 60% of the run price (Elon). Gross margin is positive by 2027-04-30 (month 6) (Bezos).
5. **Concentration.** No single customer is more than 40% of revenue at 2027-10-30 (month 12) (Buffett).

Conditions 2 and 3 must clear before the startup signs any lease, hires beyond the founding team, or spends more than $100k of the $750k.

## Next steps

1. This week: book 10 calls with hospital lab and pharmacy logistics heads and 5 calls with certified operators. Ask what each pays to move samples today and what a run would cost.
2. By 2026-12-29: operator subcontract signed.
3. By 2027-01-28: two paid pilots signed. By 2027-03-31: first paid run delivered, dispatched by hand (Graham, pulled forward).

**Cheapest test:** Before writing code, get one health system to sign a paid pilot letter at $50 or more per run, and one operator to quote $25 or less per run in writing.

**Kill criteria:** No signed operator subcontract by 2026-12-29, or no paid pilot by 2027-01-28. Then stop A. Keep the cash and bring the council back with what the calls showed.
